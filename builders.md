## Builder types (three cards)

### Bridge builders
To the AI frontier. Plugged into the one-person unicorns and the agentic edge, where AI-native companies find product-market fit before their first funding round. We filter the signal from the noise, and bring it to you.

### Community builders
Tech and AI communities inside Deutsche Telekom. Communities of adaptive leaders across many organisations. Adoption is social, so we build the social structures it needs.

### Product builders
Online and offline. Enterprise platforms, agentic systems run daily on our own infrastructure, and once, a burger van called Mother Clucker. We ship, and it shows.
