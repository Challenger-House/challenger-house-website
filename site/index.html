Zone 03 / The insight

The imagination gap.

The ROI on AI stays elusive as long as the people deciding have not sat with it themselves. The real constraint is not budget or talent. It is imagination. Nobody asks AI for something they have never seen it do. See more, and you ask for more.

S.01

What you cannot see yetUnknown unknowns

You cannot ask for what you have never seen. Most teams do not know what they are missing, because they have never watched AI work on anything that looks like their own work.

S.02

Once you have seen it, you know what you do not yet know. The questions get sharper, the gap gets specific, and the business case starts to take its own shape.

S.03

What felt strange becomes ordinary. The capability is simply how the team works now, and it stays, because the team built it rather than bought it.

The knowledge economy pays for knowing, and AI just made most knowing cheap. We build what stays scarce: judgment, know-how, trust. Sovereignty, earned only by doing the work.
