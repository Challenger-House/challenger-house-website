Zone 04 / The elephant in the room

AI is coming for the management function.

Every market leader in history rationalised waiting, and every one was being sensible, right up until it was too late. This time the dilemma is aimed at how you manage. The work below you accelerates. The management system still runs on the Monday jour fixe, the hundred-slide deck, the meeting about the meeting.

That system was built for a different era, when talking was how knowledge moved through an organisation. Now knowledge moves through AI. The talking is in the way, and it has a price.
