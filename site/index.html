Zone 02 / The missing middle

The missing middle.

You run two organisations. The formal one you wrote down, and the informal one that actually gets the work done. AI only knows the first. In between sits everything your company runs on, and nobody built it. Three gaps:

01

Tacit knowledge.

How decisions really get made. How handovers really work. Where judgment really sits. The tacit knowledge of your best people, in their heads and in the hand-offs between them. None of it is encoded, so AI cannot touch it, and every licence you bought is reading the org chart instead.

02

Organisational shadow.

Nobody skipped the writing by accident. This is the organisational shadow: the people and the politics. Ambiguity protects options, positions and peace. That is why this is leadership work with your team in the room, not a documentation project you can delegate.

03

Learning loop.

Every AI pilot reveals something true about how your company actually works. Without a learning loop, none of it is captured, every pilot starts from zero, and nothing compounds.

We build the missing middle. Own the learning loop: it matters more than any model or vendor you will ever pick.

Zone 05 / The elephant

AI is coming for the management function.

Every market leader in history rationalised waiting, and every one was being sensible, right up until it was too late. This time the dilemma is not aimed at your product or your factory. It is aimed at how you manage.

The work below you keeps accelerating. The management system does not. It still runs on the Monday jour fixe, the hundred-slide deck, the meeting about the meeting.

That system was built for a different era, when talking was how knowledge moved through an organisation. AI moves it now. The talking is in the way, and it has a price.

AI-native firms run 25% smaller, with 15% fewer managers, at comparable valuations.* And it is no longer just researchers saying it: COOs now say in public that the leadership team, not the technology, is the primary constraint on the transformation it claims to lead.**

You cannot slide-deck your way out of this one. You have to rebuild the work.
