One day. Your leadership team. Your real work.
AI live in the room.

AI value is decided at the level of the organisation, its people and its culture. This day proves it on your own work. Two reveals sit on a single value stream your team works together: a structural one and a human one. Building comes after both.

Is / Is not

IS

What it is

A strategic working day on your team’s real value creation. You leave understanding AI far better, convinced that adoption only succeeds when it is deeply interwoven with organisational development, and with the appetite to go further.

IS NOT

What it is not

People arrive expecting an AI training. The mark of a day done right is that nobody afterwards would describe it as one.

One day, whole, together. This day can no more be chopped into pieces than your organisation can. It is serious leadership work on how your organisation will run. It asks for participants, and you will work. That is the point.

A filter, stated plainly. If your leadership team cannot give this one day of undivided attention to AI, we are the wrong call. Undivided time is the intervention, and what a leadership team makes time for is the signal everyone else reads.

Zone 02 / What you leave with

Leadership outcomes.

A.01

The courage to lead AI adoption now.

As the adaptive process it actually is, and even without AI in your job title. This is the biggest organisational design question of the decade, and it belongs to whoever feels compelled to lead it.

The business case to go beyond Copilot.

Felt in your own hands. You experience your workflow without AI, with your current stack, and with frontier tools and agentic workflows. The delta becomes undeniable, and it becomes your argument to take upstairs.

Zone 03 / The methodology

The four ways of knowing.

Most organisations only practise one of four ways of knowing, and AI is about to make that one free. The day lives in the space AI opens up.

P.01

PropositionalKnowing that

Facts, frameworks, claims. Reports, slides, analyses. This is where most corporate time goes, and every AI tool automates it. Its scarcity is disappearing, which means everything you will be paid for from here sits in the other three.

AI replaces this

P.02

ProceduralKnowing how

Skills built through repetition. How to brief AI, how to iterate, how to decompose a complex challenge into delegable tasks. Built through practice on your real work.

Built in the room

P.03

PerspectivalKnowing what matters

Judgment. Seeing what AI got wrong. Knowing when an artefact will land with a stakeholder and when it will be rejected. The most valuable, and the hardest to develop.

The judgment work

P.04

ParticipatoryKnowing through co-creation

Trust through co-creation. Identity reshaped by what you build with whom. It only happens face to face, under real pressure, and it decides whether anything survives Monday morning.

The human layer

Zone 04 / The deeper outcome

You leave more sovereign.

Sovereignty in its older sense, the capacity to govern something you genuinely understand. By the end of the day you read AI on four levels at once, what you use it for, how far it reaches, what it costs, and what it quietly does to your own judgment. You leave governing the machine, rather than being governed by it.

Judgment is a muscle. Build it, and fluency with AI follows. Skip it, and convergence is all you get.
