Zone 02 / The arc of the Build

Phase 01

Before · weeks 1 to 4Define

  • Your backlog turned into defined use cases, the story built, the stakeholders mapped
  • 1:1 work with every participant at the Drawing Board, where people tell us what they would never say in front of peers and boss
  • The baseline taken: your Talking Tax, where the hours actually go, how long the work really takes

Whatever changes over the quarter gets measured against week one, not against a feeling. The Residency only works because of what happens before it.

Phase 02 · Core

The core · 3 daysThe Residency

Three days with your intact team, off the treadmill, on the workflows you named in the 1-Day. You bring the real problem and the real data. We bring frontier AI into the room and rebuild those workflows with you, live, until your team can run them without us. A month of work in three days, and the speed turns out to be the smallest part of it.

Phase 03

After · weeks 5 to 12Embed

New ways of working that actually work draw attention. Budgets get questioned, credit gets contested, and the people it unsettles look for reasons to slow it down. We stay with you through it, and it is part of the quarter, not billed on top:

  • Office hours, masterclasses, ambassador roles, weekly rhythms
  • Measurement of what changed against your week-one baseline
  • A sparring partner alongside your sponsor for the conversations that decide whether good first results survive your organisational immune system

The lesson we keep relearning: if the sponsor is not the most senior person in the room, the immune system wins by default. Adoption is what happens after the Residency.

Zone 04 / Why residential

AI gives you speed. The house gives you depth.

AI does not arrive one department at a time. It lands everywhere at once, cutting across every function. You cannot lead that from a calendar of back-to-back meetings and sequential sign-offs, one function at a time. At some point the whole team has to be in one place, at the same time, for long enough to redesign how it works together. That is the Residency.

And because AI compresses screen work so effectively, the human work can finally expand. People eat together, argue over the artefacts their AI creates, take walks between sessions, and have the 10pm conversation that changes the project’s direction.

This is why it is a house, not a workshop. A house is a container: safe enough to admit what is not working, brave enough to try the thing that might. That is what a team needs to start a complex change process, and to start it with themselves rather than with a slide about someone else.

And it is built for teams. Whether you bring a leadership team, an intact unit, or a hand-picked transformation crew, the work is the team’s, not the individual’s. AI adoption does not compound in individuals. It compounds in teams.

After the Residency, the group self-organised its next steps without being asked, ambassador roles in their home teams, and a weekly sync they set up themselves. That is what adoption taking root looks like, and it is rare.
