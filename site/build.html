Three months. Your missing middle, built inside the work.

The 1-Day ends with a backlog. The Build starts from it: the workflows worth rebuilding, the decisions that stay human, the questions the room could not answer. Three months, wrapped around a three-day Residency, to build that list into how your organisation runs.

Zone 02 / The arc of the Build

Phase 01

Before · weeks 1 to 4Define

We turn your backlog into defined use cases, build the story, map the stakeholders, and work 1:1 with every participant at the Drawing Board. People tell us individually what they would never say in front of peers and boss, and that becomes the diagnostic data the room runs on. The Residency only works because of what happens before it.

Phase 02 · Core

The core · 3 daysThe Residency

Three days with your intact team, off the treadmill, on the workflows you named in the 1-Day. You bring the real problem and the real data. We bring frontier AI into the room and rebuild those workflows with you, live, until your team can run them without us. A month of work in three days, and the speed turns out to be the smallest part of it.

Phase 03

After · weeks 5 to 12Embed

New ways of working that actually work draw attention. Budgets get questioned, credit gets contested, and the people it unsettles look for reasons to slow it down. We stay with you through it, and it is part of the quarter, not billed on top: office hours, masterclasses, ambassador roles, weekly rhythms, the measurement of what changed, and a sparring partner alongside your sponsor for the conversations that decide whether good first results survive your organisational immune system. Adoption is what happens after the Residency.

Zone 03 / What you leave with

Sovereignty, the capability, and the artefacts to prove it.

Sovereignty in its older sense, the capacity to govern something you genuinely understand: your judgment, your access, your agency, built over the quarter until it is yours to keep. It shows as adoption that takes root.

What your team walks away being able to do

01

Capability that outlasts us

Your team can take a real workflow, rebuild it with AI inside it, and keep going without us in the room. Not a demo they watched, a way of working they now own.

02

Judgment, not just fluency

They can tell where AI earns its place and where it quietly erodes the decision. They route work on purpose, pressure-test fluent output, and keep the call that stays human.

Energy that self-organises

Zone 04 / Why residential

AI gives you speed. The house gives you depth.

Because AI compresses screen work so effectively, the human work can finally expand. People eat together, argue over the artefacts their AI creates, take walks between sessions, and have the 10pm conversation that changes the project’s direction.

This is why it is a house, not a workshop. A house is a container: safe enough to admit what is not working, brave enough to try the thing that might. That is what a team needs to start a complex change process, and to start it with themselves rather than with a slide about someone else.

And it is built for teams. Whether you bring a leadership team, an intact unit, or a hand-picked transformation crew, the work is the team’s, not the individual’s. AI adoption does not compound in individuals. It compounds in teams.

After the Residency, the group self-organised its next steps without being asked, ambassador roles in their home teams, and a weekly sync they set up themselves. That is what adoption taking root looks like, and it is rare.
