Zone 02 / The founders

Marcus Druen

Founder · Organisational Architect

LinkedIn →

25 years as an OD practitioner, facilitator and coach inside blue chips including Microsoft, Telefónica, Sanofi and E.ON, with advisory work alongside ventures such as Xempus and Desolenator. He built Challenger House because the pattern repeated everywhere he looked: the system slows the very people it hired to move fast.

His craft is making the tacit explicit and the messy middle visible, so a team can actually work with what it finds. He reads AI not as a tool problem but as an organisational design problem, and he does the inner work himself, because you cannot guide people through a transition you have not made.

  • 25 years in organisational development, facilitation and executive coaching.
  • Inside the leadership systems of Microsoft, Telefónica, Sanofi and E.ON.
  • Advisory work with ventures including Xempus and Desolenator.
  • Designed and led the E.ON 1-Day executive pilot.

Will challenge you on treating AI as a tool rollout.

André Cramer

Chief Learning & AI Experience Designer

LinkedIn → DRANBLEIBEN →

25+ years in tech, from Silicon Valley (Yahoo!) to Deutsche Telekom, where he led Strategic Communications and the Think Tank for the CTIO board member and co-founded a 500-person community for responsible technology. His work focuses on what AI does to judgment and decision quality.

He developed the Plausibility Illusion framework and the cognitive-effects model that lets leaders experience how AI shifts their decisions before they are taught why. He is a keynote speaker on AI and human-centred leadership, and curator of the DRANBLEIBEN newsletter and podcast.

  • 25+ years in tech, Silicon Valley (Yahoo!) through to Deutsche Telekom.
  • Led Strategic Communications and the Think Tank for the CTIO board member.
  • Co-founded a 500-person community for responsible technology.
  • Author of the Plausibility Illusion framework; designed the E.ON "Case" exercise.

Will challenge your rose-tinted glasses, your judgment of what AI is actually good for, and where the risks live.
