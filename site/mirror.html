Zone 01 / The Org Mirror

See your organisation the way AI sees it.

AI is like an MRI scan. It doesn’t change the body, it makes visible what was always there. The Mirror turns that into an instrument: windows into your culture, your workflows, your handovers, read through lenses your organisation has never had.

Zone 02 / Why a mirror

Naming it is the first step to fixing it.

The tacit knowledge is in the attic. The shadow is in the basement. Everyone in your building knows both are there, and almost nobody has language for them. The Mirror gives you the language, grounded in your own material, your transcripts, your documents, your real ways of working, read through organisational lenses that took decades to develop and days to apply.
