Zone 02 / The dilemma

The mother of all innovator’s dilemmas.

This one is not aimed at your product or your factory. It is aimed at how you manage.

And here is the catch: you cannot act on capabilities you have never experienced. So the more senior you are, the further you sit from the tools, and the less you know what you are asking your organisation to do. You don’t know what you don’t know, and no briefing paper can fix that.

You can delegate expertise. You can’t delegate understanding.

The direction of travel is published. AI-native firms run 25% smaller, with 15% fewer managers, at comparable valuations.* And it is no longer just researchers saying it: COOs now say in public that the leadership team, not the technology, is the primary constraint on the transformation it claims to lead.**

Nobody is coming to fix management for you. Doing is the only way in. The safest place to do it is on your own work, with your own team, in a room built for exactly this. That is the day.

Zone 03 / The methodology

The four ways of knowing.

AI is about to make facts free.
We design your day so you experience all four.

P.01

PropositionalKnowing that

Facts, frameworks, claims. Reports, slides, analyses. This is where most corporate time goes, and every AI tool automates it. Its scarcity is disappearing, which means everything you will be paid for from here sits in the other three.

AI replaces this

P.02

ProceduralKnowing how

Skills built through repetition. How to brief AI, how to iterate, how to decompose a complex challenge into delegable tasks. Built through practice on your real work.

Built in the room

P.03

PerspectivalKnowing what matters

Judgment. Seeing what AI got wrong. Knowing when an artefact will land with a stakeholder and when it will be rejected. The most valuable, and the hardest to develop.

The judgment work

P.04

ParticipatoryKnowing through co-creation

Trust through co-creation. Identity reshaped by what you build with whom. It only happens face to face, under real pressure, and it decides whether anything survives Monday morning.

Zone 04 / What you leave with

Leadership outcomes.

01

The courage to redesign how you manage, not just what you use.

The courage to treat this as the adaptive challenge it is, not a technical one you can delegate. Adaptive work asks people to change what they believe and how they see their own role, and it cannot be trained into them, it has to be led. This is the biggest organisational design question of the decade. If the top does not use it, the top cannot judge it.

02

The business case to go beyond Copilot.

If AI means Copilot, the ambition starts too low. You experience your workflow without AI, with your current stack, and with frontier tools and agentic workflows. The delta becomes undeniable, and it becomes your argument to take upstairs.

We start with the stack you already own and push it past where your team thought it went. Then we put frontier tools and agentic workflows in the room, so you can judge the difference yourself and decide what is worth buying.

03

You leave more sovereign.

Sovereignty means you can govern what you understand. By the end of the day you read AI on four levels at once, and leave governing the machine, rather than being governed by it.
