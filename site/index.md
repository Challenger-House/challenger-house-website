## The missing middle

You run two organisations. The formal one you wrote down, and the informal one that actually gets the work done. AI only knows the first. Between them sits everything your company runs on but never built. Three floors, all empty:

- **Never written down.** How decisions really get made. How handovers really work. Where judgment really sits. None of it is encoded, so AI cannot touch it, and every licence you bought is reading the org chart instead.
- **Unwritten for good reasons.** Nobody skipped the writing by accident. This is the organisational shadow: the people and the politics. Ambiguity protects options, positions and peace.
- **Never remembered.** Every AI pilot reveals something true about how your company actually works. Without a learning loop, none of it is captured, every pilot starts from zero, and nothing compounds.

We build the missing middle: your organisation written down, honestly enough for your people to recognise it, precisely enough for AI to run on it, and with the learning loop that keeps it alive.

## The challenge

AI is the mother of all innovator's dilemmas, and this one is aimed at the way you manage, not your product or your factory. It opens in three places:

- **The imagination gap.** You cannot act on capabilities you have never experienced. For most leadership teams, generative AI means Copilot, so the ambition starts too low.
- **The decision gap.** The work below you keeps accelerating. The management system does not. Management has been digitised, not yet redesigned for AI.
- **The operating gap.** Your two organisations, formal and informal, drift further apart with every pilot. What each pilot reveals is never captured, so nothing compounds, licences sit idle, and pilots die in meetings.

You cannot slide-deck your way out of a dilemma. You have to rebuild the work.
