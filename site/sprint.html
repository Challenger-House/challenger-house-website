Zone 02 / The arc

PH.01

Before · weeks 1 to 4Define

We define the use cases with you, build the story, map the stakeholders, and work 1:1 with every participant at the Drawing Board. PH.02 — CORE

The core · 3 daysThe Residency

Your team, in one place, on your real problems and your real data. A month’s work in three days, and somewhere in the middle the realisation that the speed isn’t the point.

Zone 03 / What clients walk away with

Exemplary deliverables from previous Sprints.

  • Three priority workflows identified, rebuilt and running with AI inside them, chosen by the team, not by us.
  • Handover packages accelerated from months to days, with the receiving side pressure-testing them in the room.
  • A clickable KPI dashboard mockup built by the team itself, interfacing with their existing tooling.
  • 3 to 5x reported speed on research, drafts and mockups, and a backlog cleared that had been immovable for months.
  • Self-organised ambassador roles and a weekly sync the team set up without being asked. Adoption taking root, not being rolled out.
  • Board-ready artefacts: working demos, cases, maps and multimedia, not proposals about what could be built.

Zone 04 / Why residential

AI gives you speed. The house gives you depth.

Because AI compresses screen work so effectively, the human work can finally expand. People eat together, argue over the artefacts their AI creates, take walks between sessions, have the conversation at 10pm that changes the project direction. This is not a workshop where people sit around tables. It is a house. You come here to challenge yourself, challenge each other, and challenge the way you have been working.

AI adoption doesn’t compound in individuals. It compounds in teams.

After the Residency, the group self-organised its next steps without being asked: ambassador roles in their home teams, and a weekly sync they set up themselves. That is not a typical result. That is adoption taking root.
Engagement retrospective, first client
